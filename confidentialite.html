Privacy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This website

This is a personal portfolio. It has no account, no form, no cookies and no analytics, and it does not collect any personal data. For display, some pages load scripts, fonts and icons from public content delivery networks (jsDelivr, unpkg), and the site is delivered through Cloudflare; as with any website, these providers may see your IP address when a page is loaded.

The private budget application

A separate application hosted on this domain is a private tool that I use only for my own personal finances. It is not a public service and it has no other users. Access is restricted to me by a Google sign-in, and everyone else is refused.

  • What it reads: balances and transactions of my own bank accounts, in read-only mode, through the Enable Banking account information service. It cannot initiate payments.
  • Where the data is kept: only on a server that I own and operate. A daily backup is stored in my own private cloud storage.
  • Sharing: the data is never sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising or profiling.
  • Retention and withdrawal: I can end the bank connection at any time from my bank or from Enable Banking, and I can delete the data at any time.
